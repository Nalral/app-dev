# My Favorite Movie: *A Dog's Purpose*

## Overview
**Title:** A Dog's Purpose  
**Genre:** Adventure, Comedy, Drama  
**Director:** Lasse Hallström  
**Release Year:** 2017  

## Why I Love It
- It beautifully shows the bond between humans and dogs.  
- The story is emotional and heartwarming.  
- It teaches about loyalty, love, and the meaning of life — from a dog’s perspective.  

## Memorable Quote
> “My purpose, my whole life, had been to love him and be with him, to make him happy. I didn’t want to cause any unhappiness now—in that way, I decided it was probably better than he wasn’t here to see this, though I missed him so much at that moment the ache of it was as bad as the strange pains in my belly.”

## Summary
*A Dog’s Purpose* follows a devoted dog who is reincarnated several times over five decades and finds new purpose with each life. Through each journey, the dog learns what it truly means to live, love, and make humans happy.

## Rating
**My Rating:** ★★★★★ (5/5)
